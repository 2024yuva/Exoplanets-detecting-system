# Habitable Zone

## What is the habitable zone?

The habitable zone is the region around a star where conditions could potentially allow liquid water to exist on a planet's surface.

The location of the habitable zone depends on the properties of the host star.

## Extremely important distinction

Being in the habitable zone does NOT mean that:

- Life has been detected.
- Liquid water has been confirmed.
- The planet is Earth-like.
- The planet is safe for humans.
- The planet definitely has a suitable atmosphere.

A planet needs many other conditions to be suitable for life as we know it.

## Better wording

Prefer:

"potentially habitable"

or

"located in the star's habitable zone"

Avoid:

"this planet can support life"

unless the statement is specifically presented as a scientific possibility with appropriate uncertainty.

## ExoScope AI rule

When a user asks whether a planet can support life, explain the evidence and uncertainty rather than giving a simple yes/no answer.
