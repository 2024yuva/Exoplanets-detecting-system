# Exoplanet Glossary

## Exoplanet
A planet orbiting a star other than the Sun.

## Host star
The star that a planet orbits.

## Transit
The passage of a planet in front of its star from the observer's point of view.

## Light curve
A graph of measured brightness or flux versus time.

## Orbital period
The time required for an object to complete one orbit.

## Radius
The distance from the center of a roughly spherical object to its surface.

## Earth radius
A unit used to compare planet radii with Earth's radius.

## Earth mass
A unit used to compare planet masses with Earth's mass.

## Super-Earth
A planet in a broad size/mass category larger or more massive than Earth but generally smaller than the Solar System's ice giants. It does not mean Earth-like.

## Terrestrial
A rocky planet type.

## Neptunian
A planet broadly in the size category of Neptune.

## Gas giant
A very large planet with a thick gaseous envelope.

## Habitable zone
The region around a star where conditions could potentially allow liquid water on a planet's surface.

## Radial velocity
A measurement of motion toward or away from an observer, used to detect stellar motion caused by orbiting planets.

## Spectroscopy
The study of light separated into wavelengths to learn about matter and physical conditions.

## Light-year
A unit of distance equal to the distance light travels in one year.

## Transit spectroscopy
Analysis of starlight that passes through an exoplanet's atmosphere during a transit.
