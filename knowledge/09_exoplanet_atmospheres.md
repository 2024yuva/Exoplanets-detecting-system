# Exoplanet Atmospheres

## What is an atmosphere?

An atmosphere is a layer of gases surrounding a planet.

The composition and structure of an atmosphere can affect temperature, chemistry and the conditions at the planet's surface.

## Transit spectroscopy

During a transit, some starlight can pass through the planet's atmosphere.

Molecules absorb particular wavelengths of light.

By studying the spectrum, astronomers can infer the presence of certain atmospheric molecules.

## Important limitation

Detecting a molecule is not automatically evidence of life.

For example, water vapor can have non-biological origins and does not by itself prove that a planet has liquid surface water or life.

## K2-18 b example

K2-18 b has been studied using observations of its atmosphere. It is scientifically interesting because it lies in the habitable zone of its star and has atmospheric measurements that can help scientists investigate its nature.

The evidence should not be described as proof that K2-18 b contains life.
