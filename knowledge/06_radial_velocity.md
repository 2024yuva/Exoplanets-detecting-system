# Radial Velocity

## What is radial velocity?

A planet does not simply orbit a completely stationary star.

The gravity of the planet causes the star and planet to orbit their shared center of mass. From Earth, this can appear as a small back-and-forth motion of the star.

## How is it measured?

Astronomers can measure changes in the star's spectrum.

As the star moves toward or away from the observer, its spectral lines shift slightly because of the Doppler effect.

This produces a radial-velocity signal.

## What can it reveal?

Radial-velocity observations can provide information about:

- The presence of a planet
- The planet's orbital period
- The strength of the gravitational interaction
- A lower limit on the planet's mass in many common observing configurations

## Simple explanation

Transit method:

Planet blocks some starlight.

Radial velocity:

Planet makes its star wobble.
