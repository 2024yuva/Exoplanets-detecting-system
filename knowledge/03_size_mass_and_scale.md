# Planet Size, Mass and Scale

## Earth radius

Exoplanet sizes are often expressed in Earth radii.

Earth = 1 Earth radius (1 R Earth).

If a planet has a radius of 2.37 Earth radii, its radius is approximately 2.37 times Earth's radius.

## Earth mass

Mass is a different measurement from radius.

Earth = 1 Earth mass (1 M Earth).

A planet can have a radius larger than Earth without its mass increasing by exactly the same factor.

## Diameter

Diameter is twice the radius:

Diameter = 2 × radius

For a planet with radius 2.37 times Earth's radius, its diameter is also approximately 2.37 times Earth's diameter.

## ExoScope visualization rule

The Size Explorer should use radius ratios for the visual sphere size.

Do not use mass as though it were a physical radius. If the user switches to mass, display the numerical comparison separately.

## Artist concepts

The relative sphere size can represent the catalogued radius ratio, while the surface texture or color is an artist concept unless explicitly identified as an observed image.
