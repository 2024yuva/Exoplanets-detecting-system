# Featured Exoplanets

This file provides beginner-friendly context for planets featured in ExoScope.

## K2-18 b

K2-18 b is a planet orbiting the M-type star K2-18.

NASA's current catalog lists approximately:

- Radius: 2.37 Earth radii
- Mass: 8.92 Earth masses
- Orbital period: 32.9 days
- Discovery method: Transit
- Discovery year: 2015

It is located in the habitable zone of its star and has been studied for information about its atmosphere.

Important: habitable zone does not mean that life has been detected.

Source:
https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/k2-18-b/

## TRAPPIST-1 e

TRAPPIST-1 e is a roughly Earth-sized planet in the TRAPPIST-1 system.

NASA's catalog lists approximately:

- Radius: 0.92 Earth radii
- Mass: 0.69 Earth masses
- Orbital period: 6.1 days

Source:
https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/trappist-1-e/

## Proxima Centauri b

Proxima Centauri b orbits Proxima Centauri, the nearest known stellar neighbor to the Sun.

NASA's catalog lists approximately:

- Radius: 1.02 Earth radii (estimated)
- Mass: 1.06 Earth masses
- Orbital period: 11.2 days

Source:
https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/proxima-centauri-b/

## Kepler-22 b

Kepler-22 b is an exoplanet discovered using the transit method. It is notable because its orbit places it within the habitable zone of its Sun-like host star.

Source:
https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/kepler-22-b/
