# Other Exoplanet Detection Methods

## Gravitational microlensing

A foreground star and its planet can temporarily magnify the light from a more distant background star.

The planet can produce a small additional feature in the magnification signal.

Microlensing can detect planets that are difficult to find with some other techniques.

## Direct imaging

Direct imaging attempts to detect light from the planet itself.

This is difficult because a host star can be enormously brighter than its planet.

Specialized instruments and observing techniques can reduce the star's overwhelming light.

## Transit spectroscopy

When a planet transits its star, some starlight can pass through the planet's atmosphere.

Different molecules can absorb specific wavelengths of light.

Astronomers can study these spectral signatures to infer atmospheric composition.

## Important distinction

Detection methods provide different kinds of information. No single method automatically gives a complete description of an exoplanet.
